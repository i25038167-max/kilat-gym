KILAT GYM — Design Plan

ICT2215 Assessment 2 · Theme: Fitness Centre · Style: Neo-brutalism / gym poster

1. Target audience

University students and young working adults (18–30) in Cyberjaya who want an affordable, no-nonsense gym that is open late. They mostly browse on their phones, so the layout must work well on small screens. The look is loud and energetic, like a gig poster, to stand out from the calm, glossy style most gyms use.

2. Colour palette

Ink#0E0E0E
Backgrounds, borders, hard shadows

Volt#C6FF00
Main accent: buttons, highlights, numbers

Flame#FF4D1C
Second accent: tags, video box, badge

Paper#F4F1E8
Light sections, text on black

Grey#2A2A2A
Class cards on dark section

Why: volt green on black gives very strong contrast and feels like electricity ("kilat" = lightning). Orange is used only as a small second accent so it does not compete. Sections alternate black and paper so the page has a clear rhythm.

4. CRAP design principles

Contrast: black vs volt green; huge Anton headings vs small body text; featured plan in green vs other plans in paper.
Repetition: same 4px black border + hard shadow on images, cards, video and form; numbered section titles (02, 03, 04) repeat the nav numbers; same icon box style.
Alignment: every section uses the same 5% side padding and CSS grid, so text and images line up on one left edge.
Proximity: related items are grouped: class image + title + time in one card; each icon sits next to its contact detail; form label right above its input.

5. Responsive plan

  • Below 850px: all grids become one column; stats stack; nav wraps under the logo.
  • Images and video use max-width:100% so they shrink to fit; no sideways scrolling.
