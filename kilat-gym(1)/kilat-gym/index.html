02 About us

Built for students, shift workers and anyone who hates excuses.

KILAT GYM opened in 2024 in a converted warehouse near the Cyberjaya tech park. "Kilat" means lightning in Malay: fast, loud and full of energy, just like our training floor.

  1. Real coaching. Every new member gets a 1-to-1 movement check.
  2. Fair prices. Student plans from RM69 a month.
  3. Open all night. Train after class or after your shift.
